Lesson 01 · Foundation

Building Your Local Footprint

⏱ 20 mins 📍 Strategy
Local visibility is not about being famous. It is about being the first name that comes to mind when someone in your city thinks "I need a realtor." This lesson defines your visibility zone and sets the foundation for everything else.
🧠 Neurodivergent-Friendly
This lesson is about strategy — nothing to set up yet. Read it, do the exercises, then take a break before moving on. Each exercise takes 5–10 minutes max.

What "Local Visibility" Actually Means

Most realtors try to be visible everywhere. That is exactly why they are visible nowhere. Local visibility means dominating a specific geography so thoroughly that within your zone, you are known, trusted, and recommended.

Think about the most successful realtor you know personally. Chances are, they are not the most creative poster or the best-dressed person at showings. They are the one whose name comes up in conversations. That is the result of local visibility done right.

Exercise 1: Define Your Visibility Zone

Your visibility zone is the geographic area you will dominate. It should be specific enough to be ownable, and large enough to contain enough buyers.

1
Choose 1–2 neighbourhoods or districts Not a whole city. Not Lagos. Pick Lekki Phase 1, or Jabi, or GRA Port Harcourt. Specific enough to build a reputation.
2
Identify your buyer type in that zone Are most buyers in your zone investors? First-time buyers? Diaspora? Knowing this shapes every other decision.
3
Audit existing realtors in that zone Who already operates there? What are they known for? Where are they weak? Your opportunity lives in their gap.
Your Visibility Zone Statement:
"I am becoming the most trusted realtor in                 for                buyers looking for             ."
Write this in your notes app or notebook right now. This becomes your north star.

Lesson 02 · Infrastructure

Google Business Profile Setup

⏱ 25 mins ⚙️ Setup
When someone in Lagos searches "realtor in Lekki" or "buy apartment Abuja", Google decides who appears. Your Google Business Profile is a free, powerful tool that puts you in those results — and most Nigerian realtors have not set it up.
🧠 Neurodivergent-Friendly
This lesson has actual setup steps. Do them one at a time. If you get stuck on a step, skip it and come back. The most important steps are marked with ⭐.

Why This Matters

Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business) is free. It shows your contact info, reviews, photos, and services in Google Search and Google Maps. When someone searches for a realtor near them, a complete profile with reviews outranks most websites.

For a Nigerian realtor targeting a specific area, this is arguably your highest-return 30-minute investment.

⭐ Step-by-Step Setup

1
Go to business.google.com Sign in with your professional Gmail address (not a personal one). Click "Manage Now".
2
Search for your business name If it exists, claim it. If not, create a new profile. Business name format: "[Your Name] — Realtor [City]" e.g. "Chidi Okonkwo — Realtor Abuja"
3
Choose category: Real Estate Agent Primary category must be "Real Estate Agent". Add secondary categories: "Real Estate Agency", "Property Management Company" if relevant.
4
Add your service area (not a physical address) If you do not have an office, select "I serve customers at their locations" and add your target neighbourhoods and city as service areas.
5
Complete the profile fully Phone number, website (or Mainstack link), hours, description. Profiles that are 100% complete get 7× more clicks than incomplete ones.
6
Verify your profile Google will send a code by phone or postcard. Complete verification — your profile will not appear in searches until verified.

⭐ Your Business Description (Copy & Adapt)

📋 Google Business Description Template [Your Name] is a professional real estate agent serving [Area/City], specialising in helping [buyer type] find verified, legitimate properties without the stress of documentation fraud or unreliable listings. With [X years] of experience in the [City] property market, I personally verify every property I show — including Certificate of Occupancy, Survey Plan, and Deed of Assignment — before any client views it. Areas covered: [List your 2-3 focus areas] Services: Property search and acquisition · Document verification · Investor advisory · First-time buyer guidance Contact me directly: [Phone] | [Email]

Getting Reviews (The Fastest Visibility Boost)

Reviews are the single most powerful signal in Google local search. Five genuine 5-star reviews will move you above competitors with zero reviews, regardless of how long they have been in business.

How to Get Your First 5 Reviews

📋 Review Request WhatsApp Message Hi [Name], I hope you're well. I'm building my online presence as a realtor and would genuinely appreciate 2 minutes of your time. If you've been happy with my service, would you be willing to leave me a Google review? It really does make a difference for a small business. Here's the direct link: [Your Google Review Link] Thank you so much — it means a lot. 🙏
Getting your review link: In your Google Business dashboard, go to "Get more reviews" and copy the direct link. Share it anywhere — WhatsApp, email, Instagram bio.
✅ Lesson Action

Set up and verify your Google Business Profile today. Then send the review request message to your top 5 past clients or contacts. Even 1 review this week is a win.

Lesson 03 · Community

Becoming a Community Presence

⏱ 20 mins 🤝 Networking
Digital presence gets you found. Community presence gets you trusted. This lesson is about building a reputation in the physical and online spaces where your ideal buyer already gathers — without being salesy or annoying.
🧠 Neurodivergent-Friendly
Community networking can feel overwhelming for many people. The key: you do not need to attend every event or join every group. Choose ONE from each category below and commit to it for 30 days. Depth beats breadth.

Online Communities to Join

Your buyers are already gathering in digital communities. These are the highest-value ones for Nigerian realtors:

Community TypeExamplesYour Role
WhatsApp Property Investment GroupsLagos Property Investors, Abuja Real Estate NetworkAnswer questions, share market data — never lead with listings
Facebook GroupsNairaland property, diaspora investment groupsPost educational content, comment helpfully on property queries
LinkedIn GroupsNigeria Real Estate Professionals, Investment NetworksShare market analysis, connect with investors and developers
Neighbourhood/Estate GroupsLekki Phase 1 Residents, Maitama CommunityBe the go-to property resource — share tips, answer questions

The Community Contribution Formula

For every 10 interactions in a group: 8 should be helpful and educational, 1 should be a free resource offer, and 1 can be a soft pitch or listing. This ratio builds trust and prevents you from being removed or ignored.

Physical Community Presence

Online communities build reach. Physical presence builds the deepest trust. Here are the most effective physical channels for Nigerian realtors:

🏢
Estate Residents' Association Meetings Request to present a free 15-minute "Property Market Update" at the next meeting. No selling — just value. You leave as the neighbourhood's realtor.
⛪
Church & Religious Community Networks Many of Nigeria's highest-value property transactions start with trust built in religious communities. Offer free property advice sessions after services.
🤝
Professional Associations Join and attend meetings of the Nigerian Institution of Estate Surveyors and Valuers (NIESV), local chamber of commerce, or any professional body where your buyers gather.
🏦
Allied Professional Partnerships Build relationships with mortgage brokers, lawyers, surveyors, and interior designers who serve the same clients. Every referral from these partners costs you nothing and converts at very high rates.

The Allied Professional Pitch

📋 WhatsApp Message to Allied Professionals Hi [Name], I'm [Your Name], a realtor specialising in [area/type]. I came across your work and I think we serve a similar client base. I regularly have clients who need [their service — e.g. a property lawyer / mortgage advisor / surveyor] and I'd love to have someone I trust to refer them to. Would you be open to a quick call this week? I'm also happy to refer clients your way when the fit is right. No obligations — just two professionals building something useful.
✅ Lesson Action

This week: Join 1 online community and make 3 genuinely helpful contributions (no pitching). Send the Allied Professional message to 2 professionals who serve your ideal buyer type. Track who responds.

Lesson 04 · Sales

How to Sell and Close Faster

⏱ 30 mins 💰 Conversion
Speed in closing is not about pressure. It is about removing uncertainty at every stage. The faster you answer a buyer's unspoken fears, the faster they say yes. This lesson gives you the specific scripts and frameworks to do that.
🧠 Neurodivergent-Friendly
Sales conversations can feel unpredictable. The scripts in this lesson give you a clear structure so you always know what to say next. You do not need to memorise them — just understand the logic, and the words will come naturally.

Why Deals Stall in Nigeria

Nigerian property deals most commonly stall because of four fears: fear of documentation fraud, fear of making the wrong decision, fear of overpaying, and fear of the process being too complicated. Your job is to address all four before the buyer has to ask.

The Viewing Framework

Most realtors show properties and then wait. High-closing realtors guide the viewing with intention. Here is the structure:

1
Before you arrive: Set expectations Send a WhatsApp message 30 minutes before: "I'll meet you at [location] at [time]. I've pulled the property documents so we can review them together. Anything specific you'd like me to point out?"
2
During the viewing: Ask, don't tell Instead of listing features, ask: "What do you think of the space?" and "Is this the kind of layout that works for how you live?" Let them convince themselves.
3
Documents on site: Show, don't just tell Physically show the C of O, survey plan, and deed of assignment during the viewing. Say: "Here are the documents — you're welcome to photograph them and have your lawyer review them."
4
End with a clear next step Never leave a viewing without agreeing on a next action. "Shall I send you a full document summary tonight? And can we agree to speak by [day] after you've reviewed it?"

The Closing Conversation Scripts

When they're interested but hesitating:

📋 The Honest Close "[Name], I want to be honest with you because I think you deserve that. Based on everything you've told me, this property matches what you're looking for. I'm not going to pretend there's no other option — there are always other properties. But in [area] at this price, with these documents, this is genuinely good value right now. If you're not ready, I completely understand — and I won't pressure you. But if what's holding you back is something specific, I'd rather know so we can address it directly. What's the real hesitation?"

When they say "let me think about it":

📋 The Clarity Response "Of course — this is a big decision. Can I ask what specifically you want to think through? I ask because sometimes I can answer questions on the spot that save you a week of uncertainty. And just so you have the full picture: this property has had [X] viewing requests this week. I can't hold it without a deposit. If you'd like, I can put you first in line with a refundable expression of interest while you make your decision. Would that help?"

The Follow-Up Message (24hrs after viewing):

📋 Post-Viewing WhatsApp Hi [Name], it was great showing you [property] yesterday. I hope you got a chance to rest — property decisions are tiring! I've attached the document summary as promised. A few things worth noting: [1-2 specific positives about the property relevant to what they mentioned they cared about]. One question I forgot to ask: how important is [the thing they seemed uncertain about] to you on a scale of 1–10? Helps me know whether to look for alternatives or whether this one is still in the running. Happy to chat whenever you're ready. No rush.

Checklist: Signs a Lead is Ready to Close

  • They asked about the payment process or deposit amount
  • They asked a second viewing or requested to bring a family member
  • They asked about move-in timeline or renovation possibilities
  • They replied to a follow-up message with specific questions
  • They said "let me think" but have been engaging (reading your messages)

If 3 or more of these apply: call them, do not send a message. A phone call at this stage converts at 3× the rate of a WhatsApp text.

✅ Lesson Action

Save all three scripts to your phone's "Saved Messages" on WhatsApp. The next time a lead goes quiet after a viewing, send the Post-Viewing message within 24 hours. Track how many respond.

Lesson 05 · Implementation

Your 30-Day Local Visibility Plan

⏱ 20 mins 🗓 Planning
Everything in this course only matters if you implement it. This lesson gives you a concrete, week-by-week plan for the next 30 days. Do exactly what is listed. Nothing more. Completion beats perfection.
🧠 Neurodivergent-Friendly
This plan is broken into small weekly tasks. Cross each one off as you complete it. If you miss a week, do not restart from the beginning — just pick up where you left off. Progress is not linear and that is okay.

Week 1: Foundation

  • Write your Visibility Zone Statement (Lesson 1 exercise)
  • Set up and verify Google Business Profile
  • Send review requests to 5 past clients or contacts
  • Update all social media bios with your visibility positioning
  • Save the 3 closing scripts to your phone

Week 2: Community

  • Join 1 online community relevant to your zone (group, not just follow)
  • Make 3 genuinely helpful contributions in that community
  • Send Allied Professional outreach to 2 complementary professionals
  • Post one educational piece of content across Instagram + Facebook
  • Send one WhatsApp status update about your market area

Week 3: Lead Activation

  • Create and share your lead magnet (use Email Framework Module 2 suggestions)
  • Follow up with every warm lead in your pipeline using the Clarity Response script
  • Post one client story or social proof piece
  • Book at least 1 discovery call from existing warm contacts
  • Review your Google Business profile — add 2 photos if you haven't

Week 4: Consolidation

  • Review your metrics: profile visits, DMs, calls booked, reviews received
  • Continue community engagement (3+ helpful posts/comments)
  • Post a market update for your specific visibility zone
  • Request 2 more Google reviews from new contacts
  • Write a brief reflection: what worked, what to double down on in Month 2
